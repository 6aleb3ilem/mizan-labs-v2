# Personas (SPEC §21.8)

| Persona | Role | Context | What "good" looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aïcha | Commercial | 30 quotes a week, mostly from her phone between site visits | A quote from an existing project in under 5 minutes; totals and milestones right the first time; sending by WhatsApp in one tap |
| Moussa | Reception | Tablet at the reception bench, gloves, noise, trucks waiting | Intake of 16 specimens in under 3 minutes; big targets; scan-first; labels printed immediately |
| Sidi | Technician | At the press, one hand free, bright room | Worksheet for 4 specimens in under 90 seconds; numeric keypad; next field on Enter; works offline and syncs later |
| Fatimetou | Supervisor | Reviews 40 runs a day; issues reports | Review queue with the draft report preview; issue in under 60 seconds; outliers and rules visible, never silent |
| Mohamed | Finance | Statements, payments, cash close | Record a payment in under 45 seconds; ageing buckets; dunning in bulk; exports the accountant accepts |
| Director | Signs, reads dashboards on the phone | Approvals and KPIs on the go | Approve a quote or sign a report from the inbox; dashboards that load in a second |
| Ahmed | Client site manager | WhatsApp, wants the PV now | Report notification with a link that opens the PDF; verify authenticity from the QR |
| Client accountant | Invoices and receipts | E-mail and the portal | Invoice PDF, statement, receipt; pay online when available |

Each persona has a locale preference (FR by default, EN for some clients), a device class
(phone / tablet / desktop) and a permission profile (default roles of Appendix T).
